Photo Studio FAQ

What is included in the photo studio rental?

Photo studio rental includes 100sqm of natural light space, multiple aesthetic sets (sofa, kitchenette, arch, velvet, cane chair), professional backdrop system, continuous lighting rigs, and props. Everything needed for your shoot is included.

Can I rent just the photo studio or podcast studio separately?

Yes, the photo studio (Floor One) and podcast suites (Floor Two) are completely separate rental options. You can book either space independently based on your needs.

What is the minimum rental duration?

Minimum rental duration is 2 hours. We also offer half-day and full-day rates for extended shoots.

Is the studio suitable for fashion and product photography?

Yes, the studio is ideal for fashion, product, brand, and editorial photography. The natural light, multiple sets, and professional lighting rigs make it perfect for any commercial or lifestyle shoot.

Can I bring my own lighting equipment?

Yes, you can bring your own equipment. The studio has professional lighting rigs available, but you're welcome to use your own setup if preferred.

How do I book the photo studio?

You can book the photo studio through our website or by contacting us directly. We offer flexible booking options including 2-hour sessions, half-day, and full-day rates.
