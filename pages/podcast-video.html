Podcast Studio FAQ

How much does it cost to rent a podcast studio in Chiang Mai?

Podcast studio rental starts at an affordable hourly rate. We offer flexible booking options including 2-hour sessions, half-day, and full-day rates. Contact us for current pricing and package deals.

What equipment is included in the podcast studio rental?

Each podcast set includes multi-camera rigs, professional audio equipment, lighting, and a sound-treated room. All sets are pre-configured and ready to record. Our technician can assist with setup and troubleshooting.

Can I rent the podcast studio without a technician?

Yes, you can self-operate the podcast studio. However, we recommend booking our on-site technician for first-time users or complex setups. The technician handles all technical aspects so you can focus on your content.

What is the minimum rental duration?

Minimum rental duration is 2 hours. We also offer half-day and full-day rates for extended sessions.

Which podcast set should I choose?

We have four distinct sets: Set A (Wicker & Warm - lifestyle/wellness), Set B (Clean & Minimal - business/tutorials), Set C (Velvet Sofa - brand interviews/personal storytelling), and Set D (Bold & Editorial - creative/branded content). Choose based on your aesthetic preference and content style.

How do I book a podcast studio session?

You can book a podcast studio session through our website or by contacting us directly. We require advance booking to ensure studio availability and quality service.
