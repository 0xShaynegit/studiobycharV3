## QUESTIONS ANSWERED

**Q: Why integrate FAQs instead of standalone page?**
A: Service-specific FAQs are more relevant to each page topic, improving topical authority signals. Multiple pages with FAQ schema = more rich snippet opportunities (18 vs 10). Better keyword clustering.

**Q: When will I see results?**
A: Indexation: 1-2 weeks. SERP changes: 3-4 weeks. Full impact: 6-8 weeks. Expect 50-70% visibility increase by month 3.

**Q: Do I need to add FAQ sections to other pages?**
A: The three service pages (studio, podcast, photography) have the highest intent and benefit most from FAQs. Other pages (portfolio, about, book) don't need FAQs.

**Q: What if one page ranks better than the FAQ deserves?**
A: FAQ snippets are competitive. If a page earns position 0, that's additional traffic. If not, your regular snippet remains. No downside.
